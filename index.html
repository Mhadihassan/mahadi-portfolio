Technical SEO Fixing

I identify and fix technical SEO problems such as indexability issues, broken links, poor heading structure, missing alt text, duplicate page problems, sitemap checks, schema basics, crawlability issues, and technical errors that affect search visibility.

On-Page SEO Optimization

I optimize website pages with better headings, keyword-focused content structure, clean URLs, image optimization, internal linking, readable page sections, and user-friendly formatting that helps both visitors and search engines understand the page.

Meta Title & Description Optimization

I write and improve SEO-friendly meta titles and meta descriptions for important pages, products, blog posts, and service pages. My focus is to improve relevance, search appearance, click-through potential, and keyword alignment.

Internal Linking Improvement

I improve internal linking by connecting related pages, service pages, blog posts, categories, and important money pages. This helps users move through the website smoothly and helps search engines discover and understand the site structure.

Local SEO & Citation Building

I support local SEO by improving business information consistency, creating citation listings, optimizing location signals, and helping local service businesses build a stronger presence in relevant directories and map-related search results.

Link Building Support

Website Speed & Technical Improvements
